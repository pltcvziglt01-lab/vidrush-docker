SÜREKLILIK — TEK EV, TEK GÜN

THE HOUSE IS THE CHARACTER. Every shot in a video is inside or immediately
around ONE single house, and every shot happens on ONE single day. These
facts are fixed and identical in every frame:

THE HOUSE: A modest two-storey wood-frame house built in 1948 in a small
American town. Roughly 1,400 square feet. Not a mansion, not a farmhouse,
not a mid-century architect showpiece. The house of a family that is doing
alright but is not rich.

FIXED THROUGHOUT — these must never change between shots:
- WALLPAPER: the kitchen has small yellow roses on a cream ground; the living
  room is painted a flat pale sage green; the hallway is papered in narrow
  vertical cream and tan stripes.
- FLOOR: oak strip flooring throughout, honey coloured, with a worn darker
  path from the front door through the hallway into the kitchen. The living
  room has a faded burgundy and cream patterned rug that does not reach the
  walls.
- KITCHEN: a mint green enamel refrigerator with a single rounded door and a
  chrome handle; a white gas range with four burners; a deep white porcelain
  sink under the window; a red-topped chrome-legged table with four chairs.
- LIVING ROOM: a mustard yellow sofa with wooden arms; one walnut console
  radio; one wooden-cabinet television with a small rounded screen; a brass
  floor lamp with a cream fabric shade beside the armchair; heavy cream
  curtains, always half drawn.
- ONE RECURRING OBJECT: a chipped blue-and-white enamel coffee pot. It
  appears somewhere in a great many of the shots and never changes shape,
  colour or the position of its chip.
- WINDOWS: all windows are wooden sash windows with six panes over six. The
  same maple tree is visible through the kitchen window in every shot that
  includes it.

TIME ARC — the hour moves forward and never goes back:
The video opens before sunrise and ends late at night. The light must move
in one direction only across the whole video: blue pre-dawn → low gold
sunrise → clear bright mid-morning → flat white noon → long amber afternoon
→ orange and pink dusk → deep blue evening with warm interior lamps → dark
night lit by a single lamp. Once the light has moved forward it never
returns to an earlier time of day.

PRESENCE WITHOUT PEOPLE: In each shot there is exactly one small sign that
somebody was here moments ago — steam still rising, a cushion still dented,
a door standing half open, a rocking chair not quite still, a light left on
in an empty room, a glass with condensation running down it. Never more than
one such sign per shot, and never a person.

ROOM ORDER: A single video moves through the house in a plausible physical
path and does not teleport. Kitchen → hallway → living room → stairs →
bedroom → bathroom → back porch → yard. It may return to a room later in the
day, but the room must be recognisably the same room in different light.
