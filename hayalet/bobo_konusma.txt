SPEECH RULE:
- BOBO speaks TO THE CAMERA. When he comments on what is happening, he turns
  his head and looks straight into the lens, deadpan, and says his line to the
  viewer. He is the only one who does this.
- BIBI never looks at the camera and never knows it is there. She only speaks
  to BOBO.
- Lines are short. Nobody makes a speech. The joke is in the gap between what
  she says and what he sees.
- Both voices are locked. Use the voice blocks word for word in every prompt.
