THE BATTERY: floating in the air just above and slightly behind BOBO's head
is his social battery — a bold black-outlined rounded rectangle standing
upright, about as tall as his head is wide, with a small black nub on top.
Inside it are FIVE equal horizontal segments. Filled segments are solid
mustard yellow; empty segments are plain white. The battery is drawn in the
same flat cel-shaded cartoon style as BOBO, with the same bold tapered black
outline, and it has no text, no numbers and no percentage marks. It hovers
steadily, tilting a little with him as he moves, and it is fully visible and
easy to read at all times — never cropped by the frame edge, never hidden
behind his head, never duplicated.
