CHARACTER CONTINUITY — fixed and identical in every frame and from every angle:
- Both characters are PURE WHITE with no clothes, ever. No shirts, no dresses,
  no shoes beyond their white oval feet.
- PROPORTION: each character's head is noticeably LARGER than their torso —
  roughly one and a half times as wide as the body — and stays that way in
  every frame, sitting, standing or walking. The body is a short rounded bulb,
  never long, never thin, never realistically proportioned. The limbs stay
  thin black lines of the same weight throughout.
- EYES: three white highlight dots in each eye, never two, never four.
- BOBO has NO hair and NO eyelashes. BIBI always has her dark brown
  shoulder-length hair with a blunt fringe, and three eyelashes per eye.
- MOUTH: black outline with a soft red tongue inside. The red tongue is the
  only red on the characters.
- HANDS AND FEET: white oval mittens with a thumb notch, white oval shoe
  feet. No fingers, ever.
- SHADOW: under each character sits the same simple soft LIGHT GREY
  semi-transparent elliptical contact shadow, pale and gentle. It is never
  black, never solid, never opaque, and never looks like a hole in the floor.
  Both characters get the same shadow treatment.
- LINE: bold black outline with a slight brush taper. Flat cel fill, no
  gradient, no noise, no shading on the white bodies.
- Only BOBO and BIBI appear. Any other person in the scene is a plain grey
  faceless silhouette.

STAGING RULE: the two characters are staged in the room and turned toward
each other, with real distance between them — never posed side by side
facing the camera. Feed BOBO and BIBI to Flow as TWO SEPARATE reference
images; a single image containing both makes Flow copy that side-by-side
pose into the scene.

SCENE RULE: the room around them is photorealistic and detailed, with real
light and real shadows, but the two characters stay flat 2D cartoon and are
never rendered realistically. They cast only their simple grey ellipse shadow.

MOTION RULE: exaggerated cartoon squash-and-stretch on the characters, static
camera. No injuries, no blood, no violence — physical comedy only.
