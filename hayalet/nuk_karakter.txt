NUK = drawn in exactly the same clean 2D cartoon style as OOG — pale
cream-white skin, rounded head, slim cream-white arms and legs with bold black
outlines and simple rounded hands, bare cream-white feet, large oval white
eyes with big black pupils, a small black-outlined mouth with a red tongue.
Four differences from OOG, and only four: he is SHORTER and STOCKIER, about
three quarters of OOG's height with a rounder body; his dark brown hair is
flat and straight, cut in a blunt fringe hanging into his eyes, with none of
OOG's messy volume; his eyebrows are thicker and permanently lowered into a
doubtful frown; and he wears a single ragged brown fur piece draped over both
shoulders like a cape, with a plain fur loincloth beneath it. He almost always
carries a short knobbly wooden stick in one hand.
