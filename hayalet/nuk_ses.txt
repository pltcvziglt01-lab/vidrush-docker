NUK'S VOICE: a caveman voice — an adult male, lower, slower and flatter than
OOG's, gravelly and unimpressed. He does not speak any real language. He only
grunts short doubtful sounds, mostly a low "nuh" and a sceptical "hm". His
sounds are brief and dry and always land after a small pause, as if he thought
about it first. When he is finally impressed he lets out one long rising
"ooooh" — and that is the only time his voice goes up.
