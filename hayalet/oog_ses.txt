OOG'S VOICE: a caveman voice — an adult male, warm, loud and enthusiastic,
with a rough scratchy edge. He does not speak any real language. He only
grunts, hoots and says his own name, with huge feeling behind it. When he is
proud his voice booms and rises; when he is hurt it drops into a low whine.
Every sound is short and punchy, never a sentence, never a real word other
than "OOG". He is excitable and always completely convinced of himself.
