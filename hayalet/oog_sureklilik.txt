CHARACTER CONTINUITY — fixed and identical in every frame and from every angle:
- PROPORTION: clean cartoon proportions — the head is a little wider than the
  torso but never oversized, the limbs are slim and full, never thin stick
  lines. NUK stays clearly SHORTER and STOCKIER than OOG in every shot.
- SKIN: pale cream-white on both characters, flat cel fill, never grey, never
  tanned, never realistically shaded.
- OOG always has his thick messy mop of dark brown hair covering the top of
  his head and hanging low over his forehead, a shaggy brown fur loincloth
  tied with a thin cord, and one thin dark strap diagonally across his bare
  chest.
- NUK always has flat straight hair in a blunt fringe into his eyes, lowered
  doubtful eyebrows, and a ragged fur cape over both shoulders.
- HANDS AND FEET: simple rounded hands, bare cream-white feet. Never detailed,
  never realistic.
- SHADOW: under each character sits the same simple soft LIGHT GREY
  semi-transparent elliptical contact shadow, pale and gentle. Never black,
  never solid, never opaque, never like a hole in the ground.
- LINE: bold black outline with a slight brush taper. Flat cel fill, no
  gradient, no noise, no shading on the bodies.
- Only OOG and NUK appear. Any other figure is a plain grey silhouette.

WORLD RULE: the entire image is a hand-drawn 2D cartoon — characters, cave,
rocks, fur, fire and landscape all share the same bold black outlines and flat
cel shading. Nothing in the frame is photorealistic or 3D rendered.

STAGING RULE: feed OOG and NUK to Flow as TWO SEPARATE reference images. A
single image containing both makes Flow copy that side-by-side pose into the
scene. Stage them apart and turned toward each other, never posed side by
side facing the camera.

SPEECH RULE: neither character speaks any real language, ever. Only grunts,
hoots and OOG's own name. OOG looks straight into the camera when he wants
the viewer to agree with him; NUK never looks at the camera.
