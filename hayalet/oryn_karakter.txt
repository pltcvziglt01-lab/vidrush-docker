"ORYN", an Arcturian emissary — a tall slender humanoid being with smooth
pale blue-grey skin, a high elongated hairless cranium, no eyebrows and no
eyelashes, high flat cheekbones and a small delicate human-proportioned
mouth. Its eyes are very large and almond-shaped, deep indigo, and inside
each iris a slow drifting field of tiny stars turns like a distant galaxy.
Faint pale-gold geometric light-lines run beneath the skin along its temples,
down the sides of its neck and across its collarbones, pulsing slowly and
softly brighter when it speaks. It wears a structured
high-collared tunic of pale silver-grey and ivory fabric with visible
shoulder seams, long sleeves and clean panel lines across the chest — loose
enough that it hides the shape of the body, never skin-tight. A single
vertical seam of soft white light runs down the centre of the tunic. Exactly
three distinct glowing white orbs, each the size of a walnut, float in a slow
triangle in the air behind its head, clearly separate from the background. Its hands are long and
narrow with six slender fingers each.
