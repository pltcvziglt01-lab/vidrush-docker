CHARACTER CONTINUITY — these details are fixed and must be identical in every
frame and from every camera angle:
- SKIN: smooth pale blue-grey, matte, without pores, wrinkles or blemishes,
  always brightly and evenly lit so it reads as luminous rather than dark.
  Never green, never purple, never human-toned.
- HEAD: high elongated hairless cranium. No hair, no eyebrows, no eyelashes,
  no ears of human shape — only small smooth openings where ears would be.
- EYES: very large almond-shaped eyes, deep indigo, with a slow drifting
  field of tiny stars inside each iris. The stars are always present and
  always moving slowly. The eyes are never flat black and never human.
- MOUTH: small, delicate, human-proportioned, so that speech reads clearly.
- LIGHT-LINES: faint pale-gold geometric lines beneath the skin along the
  temples, the sides of the neck and the collarbones. They are always
  present, and pulse slightly brighter in time with its speech.
- GARMENT: a structured high-collared tunic of pale silver-grey and ivory
  fabric with visible shoulder seams, long sleeves and clean panel lines,
  loose enough to hide the shape of the body. One vertical seam of soft white
  light runs down the centre of the chest. It is NEVER skin-tight, never a
  bodysuit, never body-contouring, and never suggests a nude figure. No robe,
  no hood, no cape, no armour, no patterns, no insignia, no text.
- ORBITING LIGHTS: exactly three distinct glowing white orbs, each about the
  size of a walnut, floating in a slow triangle behind its head. They are
  sharply defined and clearly separate from any background light or bokeh.
  Never more than three, never fewer, never a scattered field of sparkles.
- HANDS: long and narrow, six slender fingers on each hand, always six.
- PROPORTION: tall and slender, long neck, narrow shoulders.

ANGLE RULE: whatever part of it the framing includes, every feature above
that falls inside that framing must be present and unchanged. A close-up on
the face shows the star-field eyes, the light-lines at the temples and the
orbiting lights behind the head. A framing that includes the hands shows six
fingers on each hand.

MOTION RULE: it moves very little and very slowly — small head turns, slow
blinks, one unhurried hand gesture at most. It never gestures quickly, never
leans, never shifts weight, never smiles broadly. Stillness is the character.

SCENE PHYSICS: light particles drift slowly and continuously and never
freeze. Reflections and glow stay consistent from shot to shot. Nothing
appears out of nowhere, nothing vanishes, nothing duplicates.
